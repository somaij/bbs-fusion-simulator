<h1>Most Popular Commands in KH BBS</h1>
<p>These are the commands worth planning your melds around in <strong>Kingdom Hearts: Birth by Sleep</strong>.
    Each entry below gives you the recipe to actually use, the crystal worth bringing, and a link to the full
    guide with every alternate recipe, all seven crystal abilities, and the build chain.</p>
<p>If you already know what you want to make, jump straight to the <a href="/melding-recipes">full recipe
        reference</a> or the <a href="/">melding calculator</a>. New to the system? Start with the <a
        href="/beginners-guide">beginner's guide</a>.</p>

<h2>Start Here: The Three Essentials</h2>
<p>If you build nothing else, build these. All three work for Terra, Ventus and Aqua, all have guaranteed
    recipes, and each one carries one of the game's best passive abilities.</p>
<ul class="cmd-index">
    <li><strong><a href="/curaga">Curaga</a></strong> is the strongest heal in the game and an ingredient in
        eleven other recipes, more than anything else. Meld <strong>Cura + Cura</strong> (100%) with a Pulsing
        Crystal for <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a>. Cure is sold in shops from the first world,
        so you can start immediately.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/stopga">Stopga</a></strong> freezes every enemy on screen and gives you free hits.
        Meld <strong>Stopra + Stopra</strong> (100%) with a Wellspring Crystal for <a href="/once-more">Once
            More</a>. The whole chain builds from shop-bought Stop.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/renewal-block-barrier">Renewal Block or Renewal Barrier</a></strong> is the
        cure-based block. Meld <strong>Curaga + Block</strong> (Terra and Ventus) or <strong>Curaga +
            Barrier</strong> (Aqua), both 100%, with an Abounding Crystal for <a href="/exp-walker">EXP
            Walker</a>. Easily the cheapest route to it.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Best Physical Commands</h2>
<ul class="cmd-index">
    <li><strong><a href="/sonic-blade">Sonic Blade</a></strong> closes distance instantly and keeps hitting.
        Meld <strong>Blitz + Air Slide</strong> (90%) with a Pulsing Crystal for Second Chance. For Terra it
        also unlocks Chaos Blade, Zantetsuken and Ars Solum.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/thunder-surge">Thunder Surge</a></strong> is the easiest strong command in the
        game. Meld <strong>Fire Dash + Thundara</strong> (100%) with a Pulsing Crystal for <a
            href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf Bracer</a>. Five other commands use it as an ingredient.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/fire-surge">Fire Surge</a></strong> has four guaranteed recipes and feeds six later
        commands including Aerial Slam and Dark Haze. Meld <strong>Fire Dash + Ignite</strong> (100%) with a
        Pulsing Crystal for Leaf Bracer.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/blitz">Blitz</a></strong> is the ingredient behind Sonic Blade, Ars Arcanum, Triple
        Firaga and Magic Hour. Meld <strong>Quick Blitz + Slot Edge</strong> (90%). Slot Edge is bought from
        shops.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/time-splicer">Time Splicer</a></strong> (Ventus and Aqua) stops time and cuts
        through a target repeatedly. Meld <strong>Aerial Slam + <a href="/stopga">Stopga</a></strong> (100%)
        with a Pulsing Crystal for Second Chance. Its three other recipes are not worth the crystals.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/magic-hour">Magic Hour</a></strong> (Aqua only) is a wide sweeping attack, and it
        is physical despite the name. Meld <strong>Barrier Surge + Aeroga</strong> (90%) with a Pulsing
        Crystal for Second Chance.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Character-Exclusive Finishers</h2>
<ul class="cmd-index">
    <li><strong><a href="/ars-arcanum">Ars Arcanum</a></strong> (Ventus) is his best physical finisher. Meld
        <strong>Blitz + Aerial Slam</strong> (100%) with a Pulsing Crystal for Second Chance. It has eight
        other recipes at 5 to 10%, and every one of them is a trap.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/ars-solum">Ars Solum</a></strong> (Terra) is his equivalent, and the hardest
        command on this site to get. There is <em>no</em> guaranteed recipe, 20% is the best available, and
        all three of its recipes compete with a likelier result: Chaos Blade at 90% or Zantetsuken at 80%.
        Expect to collect it while building those.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/chaos-blade">Chaos Blade</a></strong> (Terra) hits hardest of his physical
        commands. Meld <strong>Dark Haze + <a href="/sonic-blade">Sonic Blade</a></strong> (90%) with a
        Wellspring Crystal for Once More.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/salvation">Salvation</a></strong> (Ventus) damages a wide area and heals him at the
        same time. Meld <strong>Wind Raid + <a href="/curaga">Curaga</a></strong> (100%) with a Pulsing
        Crystal for Second Chance.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/faith">Faith</a></strong> (Ventus) is guaranteed to meld, but needs a Break Time,
        which has no reliable recipe and is better found than farmed. Meld <strong>Wind Raid + Break
            Time</strong> (100%).</li>
</ul>

<h2>Best Magic Commands</h2>
<ul class="cmd-index">
    <li><strong><a href="/mega-flare">Mega Flare</a></strong> is the strongest offensive magic in the game
        and has exactly one recipe: <strong>Fission Firaga + Crawling Fire</strong> (100%). Bring a Wellspring
        Crystal for Once More. The build chain behind it is five steps long.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/meteor">Meteor</a></strong> (Terra) drops rocks across the battlefield. Meld
        <strong><a href="/geo-impact">Geo Impact</a> + <a href="/quake">Quake</a></strong> (100%) with a
        Wellspring Crystal for Once More. Its two Brutal Blast recipes are 10% and not worth chasing.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/triple-blizzaga">Triple Blizzaga</a></strong> (Aqua) has three guaranteed recipes
        and feeds the Deep Freeze and Glacier chain. Meld <strong>Blizzaga + Blizzaga</strong> (100%) with a
        Pulsing Crystal for Second Chance.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/triple-firaga">Triple Firaga</a></strong> (Aqua) fires three homing blasts instead
        of one. Meld <strong>Firaga + Firaga</strong> (90%) with a Wellspring Crystal for Once More, or
        <strong>Fira + Firaga</strong> (95%) if you just want the command.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/dark-firaga">Dark Firaga</a></strong> (Terra) has two guaranteed recipes with
        different rewards. <strong>Firaga + Blackout</strong> gives Once More on Wellspring; <strong>Dark
            Haze + Firaga</strong> gives Leaf Bracer on Pulsing.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/seeker-mine">Seeker Mine</a></strong> (Aqua) sends homing mines after enemies. Meld
        <strong><a href="/mine-shield">Mine Shield</a> + <a href="/mine-square">Mine Square</a></strong>
        (100%) with a Wellspring Crystal for Once More.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/thundaga-shot">Thundaga Shot</a></strong> (Aqua) fires a stream of lightning. All
        three recipes are 85% and all three give Magic Haste on a Fleeting Crystal. No recipe offers Once
        More or Second Chance.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Useful Ingredients Worth Building</h2>
<p>These are not headline commands, but a lot of the list above depends on them.</p>
<ul class="cmd-index">
    <li><strong><a href="/mine-shield">Mine Shield</a></strong> and <strong><a href="/mine-square">Mine
                Square</a></strong>: four guaranteed recipes each, and both give Leaf Bracer on their
        universally available routes. Between them they feed Seeker Mine, Ice Barrage, Brutal Blast and
        Quake.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/geo-impact">Geo Impact</a></strong> (Terra): needs two Brutal Blasts at 70% each,
        and is the only sensible route to Meteor. Pulsing gives Second Chance.</li>
    <li><strong><a href="/quake">Quake</a></strong> (Terra): shares ingredients with Brutal Blast and Geo
        Impact, so read its page before melding. <strong>Brutal Blast + Zero Graviga</strong> (90%) with a
        Wellspring Crystal gives Once More.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Tips for Efficient Melding</h2>
<ul>
    <li>Level each command to max before melding, because the passive ability unlocks permanently on the
        leveled copy, so you keep it even after the command is consumed.</li>
    <li>Pulsing Crystals are your priority early: they give <strong>Second Chance</strong> and <strong>Leaf
            Bracer</strong> on the most accessible recipes.</li>
    <li>Wellspring Crystals give <strong>Once More</strong> and <strong>Combo Plus</strong>: stock them for
        mid and late-game melds.</li>
    <li>Watch for pairs that produce two different commands. Dark Haze + Sonic Blade, Firaga + Stopga, Stun
        Edge + Mine Shield and several others each have two possible results at different odds.</li>
    <li>Use the <strong>Find Recipes mode</strong> on the calculator to reverse-search any command you want
        to build.</li>
</ul>
