<h1>How to Get Mega Flare in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Mega Flare doesn't ask much of you. One recipe, can't fail, works for Terra, Ventus and Aqua. That makes
    it a nice change of pace from the commands that want you to roll dice. The catch, if you can call it
    that, is that your crystal is the only decision you get to make. Pick the right one and you walk away
    with a top-tier ability attached.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>One recipe means the crystal is your whole decision, and there's a clear winner.</p>
    <p><strong>Wellspring Crystal gives you <a href="/once-more">Once More</a>.</strong> This is the reason
        to care about Mega Flare beyond the fireworks. Once More keeps you alive through a full combo at 1
        HP instead of dying halfway through it, and it's most of what makes Proud and Critical runs
        manageable. Don't have it yet? Use this meld.</p>
    <p>Everything else, for when Once More is already covered:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Shimmering</strong> gives Fire Boost, which fits given what Mega Flare is</li>
        <li><strong>Pulsing</strong> gives Finish Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Fleeting</strong> gives Reload Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Soothing</strong> gives Damage Syphon</li>
        <li><strong>Hungry</strong> gives HP Prize Plus</li>
        <li><strong>Abounding</strong> gives EXP Chance</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Level it all the way up before you do anything else with it if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Mega Flare as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Mega Flare. It's the end of its own chain.</p>
    <p>That's actually convenient. You don't need to hoard it, and you're not going to regret spending it on
        something better later, because there is no later. Once you've made it and pulled your ability off
        the crystal, it's just a deck command. Go use it.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq" id="build-chain">
    <h3>The Full Build Chain</h3>
    <p>Both ingredients need building themselves, so here is the whole sequence from shop-bought commands up
        to Mega Flare. Everything here works for Terra, Ventus and Aqua.</p>
    <table>
        <thead>
            <tr><th>Step</th><th>Meld</th><th>Odds</th><th>Result</th></tr>
        </thead>
        <tbody>
            <tr><td data-label="Step">1</td><td data-label="Meld">Fire + Fira</td><td data-label="Odds">90%</td><td data-label="Result">Firaga</td></tr>
            <tr><td data-label="Step">2</td><td data-label="Meld">Aero + Aerora</td><td data-label="Odds">90%</td><td data-label="Result">Aeroga</td></tr>
            <tr><td data-label="Step">3</td><td data-label="Meld">Firaga + Aeroga</td><td data-label="Odds">80%</td><td data-label="Result">Fission Firaga</td></tr>
            <tr><td data-label="Step">4</td><td data-label="Meld">Firaga + <a href="/stopga">Stopga</a></td><td data-label="Odds">80%</td><td data-label="Result">Crawling Fire</td></tr>
            <tr><td data-label="Step">5</td><td data-label="Meld">Fission Firaga + Crawling Fire</td><td data-label="Odds">100%</td><td data-label="Result"><strong>Mega Flare</strong></td></tr>
        </tbody>
    </table>
    <p>You need <strong>two Firagas</strong>, one for step 3 and one for step 4. That means four Fira in
        total, so stock up before you start.</p>
    <p>There is some flexibility in the middle. Fission Firaga also melds from Fira + Aeroga or Firaga +
        Aerora, both at 80%. Crawling Fire also melds from Firaga + Slow or Firaga + Stopra, both at 80%. Use
        whichever version of the Stop or Aero line you happen to have.</p>
    <p>Aqua players should note a clash on step 4: Firaga + Stopga gives Crawling Fire at 80% but Firaga
        Burst at 20%, so you may need to repeat it.</p>
</div>
