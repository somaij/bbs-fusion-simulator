<h1>How to Get Fire Surge in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Fire Surge is a fast fire-elemental dash that closes distance and does solid damage on the way in. Useful
    enough by itself, but the real reason to build one is everything downstream: six commands use Fire Surge
    as an ingredient, including Aerial Slam and Dark Haze.</p>
<p>All four recipes are guaranteed and all four work for Terra, Ventus and Aqua, which makes this one of the
    most accessible commands on the site.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>Nothing can fail here, so choose purely on the ability.</p>
    <p><strong>Fire Dash + Ignite or Fire Strike + Fira with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf
                Bracer</a>.</strong> Two guaranteed routes to Leaf Bracer out of cheap ingredients is a good
        deal, especially early. The other two recipes give Finish Boost on Pulsing instead.</p>
    <p>For a fire-focused deck, <strong>Fire Strike + Fira</strong> gives Fire Boost on Shimmering, while
        Fire Dash + Ignite gives Fire Screen. Boost is offensive, Screen is defensive.</p>
    <p>Fire Strike + Fira is also the only one of the four that gives Magic Haste on Fleeting. The other
        three all give Attack Haste, which fits better given Fire Surge is a physical command.</p>
    <p>If you plan to turn it into Aerial Slam or Dark Haze, level it to max first or the ability goes with
        it.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Fire Surge as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Six recipes want one, which is the main argument for keeping a spare:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Aerial Slam, from Fire Surge + Aero, all three characters, 100%</li>
        <li>Dark Haze, from Fire Surge + Zero Gravity, Terra, 100%</li>
        <li>Fire Glide, from Fire Surge + Glide, Ventus, 100%</li>
        <li>Payback Surge, from Sliding Dash + Fire Surge, Aqua, 100%</li>
        <li>Firewheel, from Fire Surge + Cartwheel, Aqua, 90%</li>
        <li>Raging Storm, from Fire Surge + Cartwheel, Aqua, 10%</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Watch the last two. Fire Surge + Cartwheel gives Firewheel nine times out of ten and Raging Storm the
        rest, so chasing Raging Storm from this pair means repeating it.</p>
    <p>Aerial Slam and Dark Haze are the important ones. Aerial Slam feeds <a href="/ars-arcanum">Ars
            Arcanum</a> and <a href="/time-splicer">Time Splicer</a>, and Dark Haze feeds <a
            href="/chaos-blade">Chaos Blade</a>, <a href="/ars-solum">Ars Solum</a> and <a
            href="/dark-firaga">Dark Firaga</a>.</p>
</div>
