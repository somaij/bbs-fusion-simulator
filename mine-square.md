<h1>How to Get Mine Square in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Mine Square drops a field of stationary mines that detonate when enemies wander into them. It is the
    offensive counterpart to <a href="/mine-shield">Mine Shield</a>, and the two are usually built together
    because <a href="/seeker-mine">Seeker Mine</a> wants both.</p>
<p>All four recipes are guaranteed. Two work for everybody and two are Aqua-only.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p><strong>Fira + Stop or Aerora + Ignite with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf
                Bracer</a>.</strong> Same as with Mine Shield, the two universally available recipes are the
        ones carrying Leaf Bracer. If you are building both mine commands for Seeker Mine, that is several
        chances at it from ingredients you already needed.</p>
    <p>The Aqua-only recipes give something else on Pulsing: Fira + Barrier gives Finish Boost, Stopra +
        Barrier gives Combo F Boost.</p>
    <p>For elemental damage, Fira + Stop gives Fire Boost on Shimmering. The other three give Fire Screen or,
        in the case of Stopra + Barrier, Cure Boost.</p>
    <p>Level it before melding it away if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Mine Square as an Ingredient</h3>
    <ul>
        <li><a href="/seeker-mine">Seeker Mine</a>, from <a href="/mine-shield">Mine Shield</a> + Mine Square, Aqua, 100%</li>
        <li><a href="/seeker-mine">Seeker Mine</a> again, from Mine Square + Magnega, Aqua, 100%</li>
        <li>Ice Barrage, from Blizzaga + Mine Square, Aqua, 100%</li>
        <li>Brutal Blast, from Binding Strike + Mine Square, Terra, 70%</li>
        <li><a href="/quake">Quake</a>, from Binding Strike + Mine Square, Terra, 30%</li>
    </ul>
    <p>As with Mine Shield, Terra has a clash on the last pair. Binding Strike + Mine Square gives Brutal
        Blast at 70% or Quake at 30% and you cannot pick which. Brutal Blast is normally what you want, since
        it feeds <a href="/geo-impact">Geo Impact</a> and <a href="/meteor">Meteor</a>.</p>
    <p>The mine commands are also one of the few places Terra and Ventus get real use out of a command that
        mostly serves Aqua, since Brutal Blast is Terra-only and needs one.</p>
</div>

    for Seeker Mine, build it alongside Mine Shield and bank the Leaf Bracers as you go.</p>
