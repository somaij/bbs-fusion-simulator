<h1>How to Get Time Splicer in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Time Splicer stops time briefly and lets you cut through a target repeatedly before it can react. It is a
    strong physical finisher for <strong>Ventus and Aqua</strong>. Four recipes exist, but one is guaranteed
    and the rest are not close, so this is an easy build unless you are chasing a specific ability.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p><strong>Aerial Slam + Stopga with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a>.</strong>
        The guaranteed recipe carrying the best ability is the ideal outcome here, and it means you never
        need to touch the long shots.</p>
    <p>That recipe also gives Air Combo Plus on Wellspring, which suits a command you will mostly use in the
        air, plus Blizzard Boost on Shimmering and Damage Syphon on Soothing.</p>
    <p>One oddity worth knowing about. <strong>Barrier Surge + Wishing Edge with an Abounding Crystal gives
            <a href="/exp-walker">EXP Walker</a></strong>, and it is the only Time Splicer recipe that
        offers it. At 10% odds and Aqua only, it is a terrible way to farm EXP Walker. The <a
            href="/renewal-block-barrier">Renewal Barrier</a> route is guaranteed and much cheaper. Only take
        this one if you happen to be melding it anyway and get lucky.</p>
    <p>Level Time Splicer before melding it away if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Time Splicer as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Time Splicer, so once it is built and levelled you are free to just use it.</p>
</div>
<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>The <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a> will confirm the ability before you spend a Stopga on it.</p>
<p>Time Splicer is one of the easier high-end commands to put together. Build Aerial Slam, build Stopga,
    bring a Pulsing Crystal, and skip the three low-odds recipes entirely.</p>
