<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>The three recipes split cleanly, so choose based on what your build is short of.</p>
    <p><strong>Firaga + Firaga with a Wellspring Crystal gives <a href="/once-more">Once More</a>.</strong>
        Burning a second Firaga hurts a bit, but Once More is one of the strongest abilities you can carry
        and this is an easy way for Aqua to get hold of it.</p>
    <p><strong>Fira + Firaga or Blitz + Firaga with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf
                Bracer</a>.</strong> Both of the cheaper recipes land here, so that's your pick if Once More
        is already sorted.</p>
    <p>Building around fire damage? Fira + Firaga and Firaga + Firaga both give Fire Boost on a Shimmering
        Crystal. Blitz + Firaga gives Fire Screen instead, which is the defensive version rather than the
        offensive one.</p>
    <p>Max it out before melding it into something else if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Triple Firaga as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from it, which is a funny contrast with the ice version. <a
            href="/triple-blizzaga">Triple Blizzaga</a> feeds into both Deep Freeze and Glacier, but the
        fire one just stops here. Build it because you want it, not because it leads anywhere.</p>
</div>

<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>Since each recipe gives something different, the <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a> is worth a
    look before you spend your Firaga.</p>

<p>Triple Firaga is a solid upgrade to Aqua's fire game with a survivability ability bolted on. Use Fira +
    Firaga if you just want the command, or Firaga + Firaga with a Wellspring Crystal if Once More is still
    missing.</p>
