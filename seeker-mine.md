<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>All three can't fail, so the ability is the only thing to weigh up.</p>
    <p><strong>Mine Shield + Mine Square with a Wellspring Crystal gives <a href="/once-more">Once
                More</a>.</strong> Use this one. A 100% recipe that also hands you Once More is great value,
        and it's the only Seeker Mine route that offers it at all.</p>
    <p>The other two are nearly identical to each other. Both give Fire Screen on Shimmering, Attack Haste
        on Fleeting and Combo Plus on Wellspring. Pulsing is the only place they differ: <strong>Mine Shield
            + Magnega</strong> gives Finish Boost, while <strong>Mine Square + Magnega</strong> gives <a
            href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf Bracer</a>.</p>
    <p>So in practical terms, use Mine Shield + Mine Square for Once More, or Mine Square + Magnega for Leaf
        Bracer. Mine Shield + Magnega doesn't really have a reason to exist unless it's all you've got.</p>
    <p>Level it before melding it away if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>
