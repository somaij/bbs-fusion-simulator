<h1>How to Get Sonic Blade in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Sonic Blade is a command people build on purpose rather than stumble into. It rushes you straight at
    whatever you're fighting and keeps hitting, so it's great for bosses that like to dance around the
    arena while you flail at empty air. If you're playing Terra there's a second reason to want it: three of
    his heaviest late-game commands need it as an ingredient.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>Here's where your recipe choice actually matters. The ability is tied to the exact pair of
        ingredients, not to Sonic Blade itself, so the three routes give you three different options.</p>
    <p><strong>Blitz + Air Slide with a Pulsing Crystal gives you <a href="/second-chance">Second
                Chance</a>.</strong> If you don't have Second Chance yet, use this one and don't overthink
        it. Living through a combo at 1 HP beats any damage ability you could pick up this early.</p>
    <p><strong>Blitz + Dark Haze with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf Bracer</a></strong>
        for Terra. Good backup option once Second Chance is already handled.</p>
    <p><strong>Fire Dash + Thunder Surge with a Pulsing Crystal gives Finish Boost.</strong> Pure damage.
        Save it for when your defensive abilities are sorted out.</p>
    <p>All three recipes give Combo Plus on a Wellspring Crystal, so if a longer ground combo is what you're
        after, take whichever route is cheapest for you.</p>
    <p>Standard warning: max out Sonic Blade before you meld it into anything else, or the ability you just
        farmed goes with it.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Sonic Blade as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>If you're Terra, Sonic Blade opens up a big chunk of your endgame deck:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><a href="/chaos-blade">Chaos Blade</a>, from Dark Haze + Sonic Blade</li>
        <li>Zantetsuken, from Sonic Blade + <a href="/stopga">Stopga</a></li>
        <li><a href="/ars-solum">Ars Solum</a>, from either Dark Haze + Sonic Blade or Sonic Blade + Stopga</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Every single one of those is Terra-only. Playing Ventus or Aqua? Then Sonic Blade is the end of the
        line for you, and you can spend it freely without worrying about what you're giving up.</p>
</div>
