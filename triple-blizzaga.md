<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>Every recipe is guaranteed, so pick purely on the ability you want.</p>
    <p><strong>Blizzaga + Blizzaga with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/second-chance">Second
                Chance</a>.</strong> Two Blizzagas is the expensive version, but a meld that can't fail and
        also gives you Second Chance earns it.</p>
    <p><strong>Blitz + Blizzaga or Blizzara + Blizzaga with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a
                href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf Bracer</a>.</strong> The cheaper routes both land here. Still a
        great ability, and it costs you less.</p>
    <p>For an ice deck, Blizzara + Blizzaga and Blizzaga + Blizzaga both give Blizzard Boost on Shimmering.
        Blitz + Blizzaga gives Blizzard Screen, which is defensive rather than offensive.</p>
    <p>Planning to carry on to Deep Freeze or Glacier? <strong>Level Triple Blizzaga to max first</strong>,
        otherwise the ability you just farmed disappears with it.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Triple Blizzaga as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Here's what sets it apart from <a href="/triple-firaga">Triple Firaga</a>, which leads nowhere:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Deep Freeze, from Blizzaga + Triple Blizzaga, Aqua, 100%</li>
        <li>Glacier, from Triple Blizzaga + Deep Freeze, Aqua, 100%</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Read that carefully, because it's easy to get caught out. Glacier needs a Triple Blizzaga
        <em>and</em> a Deep Freeze, and Deep Freeze eats a Triple Blizzaga of its own. So going all the way
        to Glacier means making <strong>two</strong> Triple Blizzagas. Stock up on Blizzaga first. The whole
        chain is guaranteed, so ingredients are the only thing standing between you and Glacier.</p>
</div>
