<h1>How to Get Ars Arcanum in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Ars Arcanum is Ventus' big late-game combo command, a long flurry of hits that tears through anything
    standing still long enough to take it. Two things to get out of the way first. It's
    <strong>Ventus-only</strong>, so Terra players want Ars Solum instead and Aqua doesn't get either. And
    while the data lists nine recipes for it, you only need to care about one.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>Assuming you're using <strong>Blitz + Aerial Slam</strong> like a sensible person, here's what each
        crystal gets you.</p>
    <p><strong>Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a>.</strong> Take this if you
        don't have it. You'll be building Ars Arcanum around the same time Second Chance starts really
        mattering, so the timing works out nicely.</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Wellspring</strong> gives Air Combo Plus, which suits a command you'll mostly be using
            in the air anyway</li>
        <li><strong>Shimmering</strong> gives Blizzard Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Fleeting</strong> gives Reload Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Soothing</strong> gives Damage Syphon</li>
        <li><strong>Hungry</strong> gives HP Prize Plus</li>
        <li><strong>Abounding</strong> gives Lucky Strike</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Level it before melding it away, or you lose the ability.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Ars Arcanum as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Ars Arcanum, so there's nothing to save it for. Build it, max it out, leave it in
        your deck.</p>
    <p>Came here as Terra looking for your version? You want <strong><a href="/ars-solum">Ars Solum</a></strong>,
        which melds from Dark Haze + <a href="/sonic-blade">Sonic Blade</a> or Sonic Blade + <a href="/stopga">Stopga</a>.</p>
</div>

<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>The <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a> shows you the result and the ability before you commit, so
    you're not guessing with a rare crystal on the line.</p>

<p>Short version: play Ventus, make Blitz, add Aerial Slam, bring a Pulsing Crystal. Ars Arcanum is one of
    the best things in Ventus' kit and it costs you a single guaranteed meld. Leave the 5% recipes alone.</p>
