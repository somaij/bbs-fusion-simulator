<h1>How to Get Thundaga Shot in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Thundaga Shot fires off a stream of lightning bolts, which suits Aqua's ranged, magic-heavy style much
    better than one big cast would. It's <strong>Aqua-only</strong>. All three recipes sit at the same 85%,
    so your decision comes down to which ingredients you can live without and which ability you want out of
    it.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>With the odds tied, the ability is the only thing separating these. One nice bit of consistency
        first.</p>
    <p><strong>All three recipes give Magic Haste on a Fleeting Crystal.</strong> If that's what you're
        after, use whatever you have spare and don't think about it any harder. Magic Haste suits Aqua
        especially well, since she leans on magic more than the other two do.</p>
    <p><strong>Freeze Raid + Thundaga or Firaga + Thundaga with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a
                href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf Bracer</a>.</strong> Two of the three end up here, so it's an easy
        one to pick up. Strike Raid + Thundaga gives Combo F Boost instead.</p>
    <p>For elemental damage, match the crystal to your recipe. Strike Raid gives Thunder Boost on Shimmering,
        Freeze Raid gives Blizzard Boost, and Firaga gives Fire Boost.</p>
    <p>One thing to be aware of before you start farming: <strong>no Thundaga Shot recipe offers Once More or
            Second Chance.</strong> If you're still hunting those, look elsewhere. <a
            href="/seeker-mine">Seeker Mine</a> and <a href="/triple-blizzaga">Triple Blizzaga</a> are both
        better targets for Aqua and both are guaranteed.</p>
    <p>Level it before melding it into anything else if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Thundaga Shot as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Thundaga Shot, so there's no later recipe you'd be spoiling by using it.</p>
    <p>Worth separating this from <strong>Lightning Ray</strong>, which people mix it up with. Lightning Ray
        is a Shotlock, not a deck command, and works completely differently.</p>
</div>

<p>Thundaga Shot is a decent addition to Aqua's magic deck. Three interchangeable recipes at identical odds,
    Magic Haste guaranteed on Fleeting, and an easy path to Leaf Bracer. Just budget for a Thundaga and
    expect to fail one now and then.</p>
