<h1>How to Get Stopga in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Stopga freezes every enemy on screen at once, which turns dangerous fights into free hits. It is the best
    crowd control in the game and it works for Terra, Ventus and Aqua. Both recipes are guaranteed, and the
    whole chain builds out of Stop, which you can buy from the first world's shop.</p>
<p>It is also a heavily used ingredient. Nine recipes want a Stopga, including some of Terra's best
    commands, so make a spare.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p><strong>Stopra + Stopra with a Wellspring Crystal gives <a href="/once-more">Once More</a>.</strong>
        Worth the extra Stopra. Once More is one of the two abilities that make higher difficulties
        survivable, and this is a guaranteed recipe built out of shop-bought ingredients.</p>
    <p>That same recipe also gives Defender on Soothing and EXP Chance on Abounding, while Stop + Stopra
        gives HP Boost and Link Prize Plus in those slots instead.</p>
    <p>Both recipes share the rest: Thunder Boost on Shimmering, Combo F Boost on Pulsing, and Treasure
        Magnet on Hungry. Neither offers Second Chance, so if that is the gap in your build, look at <a
            href="/curaga">Curaga</a> instead.</p>
    <p>Level Stopga before melding it into anything else if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Stopga as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nine recipes want one, and several of them are endgame commands:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Zantetsuken, from Dark Haze + Stopga or <a href="/sonic-blade">Sonic Blade</a> + Stopga, Terra, 80%</li>
        <li><a href="/ars-solum">Ars Solum</a>, from those same two pairs, Terra, 20%</li>
        <li><a href="/time-splicer">Time Splicer</a>, from Aerial Slam + Stopga, Ventus and Aqua, 100%</li>
        <li><a href="/time-splicer">Time Splicer</a> again, from Stopga + Barrier, Aqua, 20%</li>
        <li>Crawling Fire, from Firaga + Stopga, all three characters, 80%</li>
        <li>Firaga Burst, from Firaga + Stopga, Aqua, 20%</li>
        <li>Stop Barrier, from Stopga + Barrier, Aqua, 80%</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Notice how many of those pairs are shared. Dark Haze + Stopga and Sonic Blade + Stopga each give
        Zantetsuken at 80% or Ars Solum at 20%, and Firaga + Stopga gives Crawling Fire at 80% or Firaga
        Burst at 20%. Check the simulator first if you are aiming for the rarer result.</p>
    <p>Crawling Fire matters more than it looks, since it is half of the <a href="/mega-flare">Mega Flare</a>
        recipe.</p>
</div>
<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>Several Stopga pairings produce two different commands, so the <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a>
    is worth a look before you spend one.</p>
<p>Stopga is cheap, guaranteed, available from the first world, and it feeds nine other recipes. Between the
    crowd control and the Once More on Stopra + Stopra, there is no reason to leave this one until later.</p>
