<h1>KH BBS Melding Recipes by Ability</h1>
<p>In <strong>Kingdom Hearts: Birth by Sleep</strong>, you do not meld for an ability directly. You meld a specific pair of commands, add a crystal, and that exact combination decides which passive ability the new command carries. The pair matters as much as the crystal: <strong>Cura + Cura</strong> with a Pulsing Crystal gives you Second Chance, while <strong>Cure + Cura</strong> with the same crystal gives Combo F Boost. Both make Curaga.</p>

<p>So this page is organized the way you actually shop for abilities. Pick the ability you want, then take one of the exact recipes listed under it and bring the crystal named in that section.</p>

<p><strong>What is on this page:</strong> the most practical route to each ability worth planning around, which in almost every case means a guaranteed recipe on a command you would want to keep anyway. It is not an exhaustive list. There are 296 recipes in the game, and 32 different commands alone can carry Second Chance. For the complete set, use the <a href="/">melding calculator</a>, which searches in both directions. To learn how crystals and abilities work in the first place, start with the <a href="/beginners-guide">beginner's guide</a> or the <a href="/crystals">crystal guide</a>.</p>

<h2>The Abilities You Do Not Need to Plan For</h2>
<p>Not every ability is worth hunting a recipe for. Combo Plus is carried by 78 different melds, Attack Haste by 68, Magic Haste and Treasure Magnet by 62 each, Reload Boost by 52. With numbers like that, whatever you were already melding will probably carry the one you want, so just add the crystal and check the result in the calculator before you commit.</p>
<p>That covers most of what the Fleeting, Hungry, Soothing and Shimmering crystals grant. The four abilities above are the exceptions worth building around. For the full breakdown of which crystal grants what, and where to farm each one, see the <a href="/crystals">crystal guide</a>.</p>
