    other commands use it as an ingredient, so having a spare around is never wasted.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>Four recipes means you get to farm the ability you actually want instead of accepting whatever the
        game hands you.</p>
    <p><strong>Fire Dash + Thundara or Freeze Raid + Thundara with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a
                href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf Bracer</a>.</strong> Two separate can't-fail recipes both drop Leaf
        Bracer, which makes this one of the easier ways to pick it up. No more getting knocked out of a Cure
        halfway through.</p>
    <p><strong>Stun Edge + Thundara or Confusion Strike + Thundara with a Fleeting Crystal gives Magic
            Haste.</strong> These two recipes have identical ability sets, so treat them as the same thing
        and use whichever ingredient you care less about.</p>
    <p>Those same two recipes give <strong>Thunder Boost</strong> on a Shimmering Crystal if you're building
        a lightning deck.</p>
    <p>Small thing that might matter to you: Fire Dash + Thundara is the only route that gives Combo Plus on
        a Wellspring Crystal. The other three all give Air Combo Plus instead. Worth checking if you're
        building around ground combos specifically.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Thunder Surge as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>This is the real reason to keep a spare. Five commands need it:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><a href="/sonic-blade">Sonic Blade</a>, from Fire Dash + Thunder Surge, for all three characters</li>
        <li>Lightning Ray, also from Fire Dash + Thunder Surge, all three characters</li>
        <li>Spark Raid, from Thunder Surge + Dodge Roll, Ventus</li>
        <li>Thunder Roll, also from Thunder Surge + Dodge Roll, Ventus</li>
        <li>Payback Surge, from Sliding Dash + Thunder Surge, Aqua</li>
    </ul>
    <p>You probably noticed the duplicates there. <strong>Fire Dash + Thunder Surge</strong> can give you
        either Sonic Blade or Lightning Ray, and <strong>Thunder Surge + Dodge Roll</strong> can give you
        either Spark Raid or Thunder Roll. Same ingredients, two possible results. Run it through the
        simulator first if you're aiming at one specifically.</p>
</div>

<p>Thunder Surge is about as low-risk as this system gets. Guaranteed recipes, works for everyone, useful in
