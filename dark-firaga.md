<h1>How to Get Dark Firaga in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Dark Firaga is Terra's dark-fire projectile and it slots neatly into the darker deck he ends up building
    later in his story. It's <strong>Terra-only</strong>, and both of its recipes are guaranteed, which
    makes it one of the more relaxed strong commands to put together.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>This is the interesting part. The two recipes have completely different ability sets, so pick your
        recipe based on which ability you're missing rather than which ingredients you have.</p>
    <p><strong>Firaga + Blackout with a Wellspring Crystal gives <a href="/once-more">Once More</a>.</strong>
        Live through a full combo at 1 HP. If Once More isn't in your build yet, this is the recipe you
        want.</p>
    <p><strong>Dark Haze + Firaga with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/leaf-bracer">Leaf Bracer</a>.</strong>
        No more getting interrupted mid-Cure. Better pick if Once More is already handled.</p>
    <p>Past those two: Firaga + Blackout also gives Fire Boost on Shimmering and Damage Syphon on Soothing,
        while Dark Haze + Firaga gives Fire Screen on Shimmering and Combo Plus on Wellspring.</p>
    <p>Level it before you meld it away if you want to keep what you farmed.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Dark Firaga as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Dark Firaga, so there's no future recipe you'd be spoiling by using it. Build it
        and enjoy it.</p>
