<div class="faq">
    <h3>Two Traps Worth Knowing About</h3>
    <p>Quake shares ingredients with three other commands, and the odds are not in your favour on any of them.</p>
    <p><strong>Brutal Blast + Brutal Blast</strong> gives <a href="/geo-impact">Geo Impact</a> at 70% and
        Quake at only 30%. Since you need both Geo Impact and Quake for Meteor, that is not a disaster, but do
        not use this pair expecting a Quake.</p>
    <p><strong>Stun Edge + Mine Shield</strong> and <strong>Binding Strike + Mine Square</strong> both give
        Brutal Blast at 70% and Quake at 30%. Again the common result is the ingredient, not the target.</p>
    <p>Going the other way, the two 90% Quake recipes also give <a href="/meteor">Meteor</a> at 10%. So if
        you already have a Geo Impact banked, a failed Quake attempt occasionally hands you the Meteor
        directly.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p><strong>Brutal Blast + Zero Graviga with a Wellspring Crystal gives <a href="/once-more">Once More</a>.</strong>
        This is the recipe to use. Best odds available and the best ability in the set. It also gives Fire
        Boost on Shimmering, Finish Boost on Pulsing and EXP Chance on Abounding.</p>
    <p>The other 90% recipe, Brutal Blast + Magnega, gives Combo Plus on Wellspring instead, so there is no
        reason to prefer it unless Zero Graviga is out of reach.</p>
    <p>Curiously, the worst-odds recipe has the second-best abilities. Brutal Blast + Brutal Blast gives <a
            href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a> on Pulsing and Cure Boost on Shimmering. But at 30% for
        Quake and 70% for Geo Impact, treat that as a Geo Impact recipe that sometimes surprises you.</p>
    <p>Level Quake before melding it into Meteor or you lose the ability.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Quake as an Ingredient</h3>
    <ul>
        <li><a href="/meteor">Meteor</a>, from <a href="/geo-impact">Geo Impact</a> + Quake, Terra, 100%</li>
        <li>Meteor Crash, from <a href="/blitz">Blitz</a> + Quake, Terra, 100%</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Both are guaranteed, and both are worth building, so consider making two Quakes. Meteor and Meteor
        Crash are different commands despite the similar names.</p>
</div>
