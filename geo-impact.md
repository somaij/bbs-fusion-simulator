<h1>How to Get Geo Impact in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Geo Impact slams the ground and hits everything around you. Decent enough on its own, but the reason
    you're probably here is what comes after it. It's the only sensible ingredient for <a
        href="/meteor">Meteor</a>, so it's a required stop on the way to one of Terra's best commands. It's
    <strong>Terra-only</strong>.</p>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>How to Meld Geo Impact (The Only Recipe)</h3>
    <p>One recipe, and it wants two of the same command:</p>
    <table>
        <thead>
            <tr>
                <th>Ingredient 1</th>
                <th>Ingredient 2</th>

                <td data-label="Ingredient 1">Brutal Blast</td>
                <td data-label="Ingredient 2">Brutal Blast</td>
                <td data-label="Odds">70%</td>
                <td data-label="Works for">Terra only</td>
                <td data-label="Simulator Link"><a
                        href="/?mode=simulator&cmd1=Brutal%20Blast&cmd2=Brutal%20Blast">Try this combo</a>
                </td>
            </tr>
        </tbody>
    </table>
    <p>Note that you need <strong>two</strong> Brutal Blasts, not one. That doubles your shopping list, and
        at 70% it isn't guaranteed either, so pick up a spare pair while you're at it. Nothing worse than
        farming two of something and losing them to a failed meld.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>One recipe means the crystal is your only decision here.</p>
    <p><strong>Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a>.</strong> Take it if you
        don't have it. You're melding Geo Impact anyway on your way to Meteor, so picking up Second Chance
        during a step you were already taking is basically free.</p>
    <p>The rest:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Shimmering</strong> gives Cure Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Fleeting</strong> gives Reload Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Wellspring</strong> gives Combo Plus</li>
        <li><strong>Soothing</strong> gives Defender</li>
        <li><strong>Hungry</strong> gives Treasure Magnet</li>
        <li><strong>Abounding</strong> gives Lucky Strike</li>
    </ul>
    <p>This one matters more than usual: if Meteor is the plan, <strong>level Geo Impact all the way
            first</strong>. Melding it away early throws out the ability you just spent a crystal on. I've
        done this and it's annoying.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Geo Impact as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Just the one, and it's the whole point:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><a href="/meteor">Meteor</a>, from Geo Impact + Quake, Terra, 100%</li>
    </ul>
    <p>That's why you'd build Geo Impact deliberately instead of tripping over it by accident. Geo Impact +
        Quake is the only guaranteed way to get Meteor. The alternatives are 10% rolls, so this is the path
        you want.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq" id="build-chain">
    <h3>Building the Brutal Blasts First</h3>
    <p>Geo Impact needs two Brutal Blasts, and Brutal Blast is Terra-only with two recipes at 70% each:</p>
    <table>
        <thead>
            <tr><th>Meld</th><th>Odds</th><th>Result</th></tr>
        </thead>
        <tbody>
            <tr><td data-label="Meld">Stun Edge + <a href="/mine-shield">Mine Shield</a></td><td data-label="Odds">70%</td><td data-label="Result">Brutal Blast</td></tr>
            <tr><td data-label="Meld">Binding Strike + <a href="/mine-square">Mine Square</a></td><td data-label="Odds">70%</td><td data-label="Result">Brutal Blast</td></tr>
        </tbody>
    </table>
    <p>Careful here: both pairs also give <a href="/quake">Quake</a> at 30% instead of Brutal Blast. Since
        you need Quake for Meteor anyway, that is not wasted, but it does mean the number of melds you need
        is unpredictable. If you are going all the way to <a href="/meteor">Meteor</a>, you want at least
        three Brutal Blasts: two for Geo Impact and one for Quake.</p>
</div>
<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>The <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a> lets you check the ability before you spend two Brutal
    Blasts finding out.</p>

<p>Geo Impact is a stepping stone, but it's a stepping stone worth doing properly. Two Brutal Blasts in, a
    Second Chance along the way, and a guaranteed route to Meteor waiting at the other end. Just remember to
    level it before you spend it.</p>
