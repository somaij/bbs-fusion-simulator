<h1>How to Get Faith in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Faith surrounds Ventus with pillars of light that damage everything nearby. Along with <a
        href="/salvation">Salvation</a> it is one of his two signature high-end commands, and it is
    <strong>Ventus-only</strong>. There is a single recipe and it cannot fail, so on paper this is easy.</p>
<p>The difficulty is not the meld. It is getting hold of a Break Time.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>The Break Time Problem</h3>
    <p>Break Time has no dependable meld recipe. It shows up as a 10% result when you meld a Curaga with
        Renewal Block, <a href="/renewal-block-barrier">Renewal Barrier</a>, Focus Block or Focus Barrier. The other 90% of the time you get Slot
        Edge instead.</p>
    <p>So melding for Break Time means burning a <a href="/curaga">Curaga</a> and a defensive command per
        attempt for a one in ten shot. Most people find it faster to pick Break Time up from treasure chests
        and enemy drops instead, then spend it here.</p>
    <p>The consolation, if you do meld for it, is that Slot Edge is genuinely useful. It is part of the <a
            href="/blitz">Blitz</a> recipe, so a failed attempt still leaves you with something worth having.</p>
    <p>You will also want <strong>two Wind Raids</strong> if you plan on making Salvation as well, since both
        commands consume one.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>One recipe, so the crystal is your only decision.</p>
    <p><strong>Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a>.</strong> Given how much
        effort a Break Time costs, you may as well get the best ability out of the meld. Take this unless you
        already have it.</p>
    <p>The rest of the set:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Shimmering</strong> gives Cure Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Fleeting</strong> gives Reload Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Wellspring</strong> gives Combo Plus</li>
        <li><strong>Soothing</strong> gives Defender</li>
        <li><strong>Hungry</strong> gives Treasure Magnet</li>
        <li><strong>Abounding</strong> gives Lucky Strike</li>
    </ul>
    <p>That is the same ability set as Salvation, which makes sense given they share the Wind Raid half of
        the recipe. Level Faith before melding it away if you want to keep what you farmed.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Faith as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Faith. Once it is built and levelled it stays in your deck.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq" id="build-chain">
    <h3>Building the Wind Raid</h3>
    <p>Wind Raid has two guaranteed recipes, both Ventus-only:</p>
    <table>
        <thead>
            <tr><th>Meld</th><th>Odds</th><th>Result</th></tr>

        <tbody>
            <tr><td data-label="Meld">Freeze Raid + Aeroga</td><td data-label="Odds">100%</td><td data-label="Result">Wind Raid</td></tr>
            <tr><td data-label="Meld">Treasure Raid + Aeroga</td><td data-label="Odds">100%</td><td data-label="Result">Wind Raid</td></tr>
        </tbody>
    </table>
    <p>Aeroga comes from Aero + Aerora at 90%. Make <strong>two Wind Raids</strong> if you want <a
            href="/salvation">Salvation</a> as well, since it needs one too.</p>
    <p>Break Time, by contrast, is best found rather than melded. It turns up in treasure chests, as an enemy
        drop, and from leveling certain commands.</p>
</div>
<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>The <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a> will confirm the ability before you spend a hard-won Break
    Time on it.</p>
<p>Faith is a guaranteed meld gated behind an awkward ingredient. Find a Break Time rather than farming one,
    keep a spare Wind Raid for Salvation, and bring a Pulsing Crystal when you finally do the meld.</p>
