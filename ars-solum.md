<h1>How to Get Ars Solum in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Ars Solum is Terra's version of what Ventus gets with <a href="/ars-arcanum">Ars Arcanum</a>, a long
    punishing combo that's one of the best things he can put in a deck. It's <strong>Terra-only</strong>,
    and it comes with a catch that most commands don't have: nothing here is guaranteed. Your best odds are
    20%. Bring spare ingredients.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Watch Out: Every Recipe Competes With Something Else</h3>
    <p>This trips people up, so read this bit before you meld anything. <strong>Dark Haze + Sonic
            Blade</strong> is listed for <a href="/chaos-blade">Chaos Blade</a> at 90% and for Ars Solum at
        20%. What that means in practice is you'll usually end up with Chaos Blade.</p>
    <p>Here is the part that makes Ars Solum genuinely awkward: <strong>all three of its 20% recipes are
            competing with something else</strong>, so there is no clean route at all.</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Dark Haze + Sonic Blade gives <a href="/chaos-blade">Chaos Blade</a> at 90%</li>
        <li>Dark Haze + Stopga gives Zantetsuken at 80%</li>
        <li>Sonic Blade + Stopga gives Zantetsuken at 80%</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Whichever pair you pick, you will mostly be making a different command. The upside is that Chaos Blade
        and Zantetsuken are both excellent Terra commands, so the failed attempts are not wasted. Treat Ars
        Solum as something you collect while building those two rather than a thing you farm directly.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>The ability comes from the exact pair you used, and the three 20% recipes split into two groups.</p>
    <p><strong>Dark Haze + Sonic Blade and Dark Haze + Stopga both give <a href="/once-more">Once More</a>
            on a Wellspring Crystal.</strong> These two share an identical ability set. Once More is the one
        worth having, since surviving a whole combo at 1 HP is exactly the problem Terra runs into on Proud
        and Critical.</p>
    <p><strong>Sonic Blade + Stopga gives <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a> on a Pulsing
            Crystal.</strong> The other half of the survivability pair, if that's the hole in your build.</p>
    <p>Three of the four 5% recipes give Finish Boost on Pulsing instead. The odd one out is Confusion
        Strike + Thundara, which gives Combo F Boost. None of the long shots offer Once More or Second
        Chance at all, which is one more reason to stay on the 20% routes.</p>
    <p>Max it out before melding it into anything else if you want to keep whatever you farmed.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Ars Solum as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Ars Solum. After all that effort getting one, you don't have to agonise over
        whether to save it. Level it and put it in your deck.</p>
</div>

<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>Given the odds on this one, checking first is a good idea. The <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a>
    shows you the result and the ability before you spend a crystal.</p>

<p>Ars Solum takes more patience than anything else on this site. No 100% route, 20% at best, and every
    recipe is competing with a command that is four to five times likelier to appear. Stock up on Dark Haze,
    Sonic Blade and Stopga, bring Wellspring Crystals, and expect to build a few Chaos Blades and
    Zantetsukens along the way.</p>
