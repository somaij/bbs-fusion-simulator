<h1>How to Get Chaos Blade in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Chaos Blade hits like a truck and it's about as simple to make as they come. One recipe, good odds, and no
    competition for the ingredients once <a href="/sonic-blade">Sonic Blade</a> is out of the way. Like most
    of Terra's heavy physical commands, it's <strong>Terra-only</strong>.</p>

        better, so it goes quickly.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>The Same Recipe Can Give You Ars Solum Instead</h3>
    <p>Something to know before you meld. <strong>Dark Haze + Sonic Blade</strong> is listed for Chaos Blade
        at 90% and for <a href="/ars-solum">Ars Solum</a> at 20%. Chaos Blade is by far the likelier result,
        so this pair is a fine place to build one. Do not assume Ars Solum has an easier route elsewhere,
        though: its other two recipes both compete with Zantetsuken at 80%, so every Ars Solum recipe is
        fighting a likelier result.</p>
    <p>If you want both eventually, this pair isn't a bad place to start. You'll get Chaos Blade most times
        and occasionally luck into the other one.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>One recipe, so the crystal is the only call you make here, and there's an easy pick.</p>
    <p><strong>Wellspring Crystal gives <a href="/once-more">Once More</a>.</strong> If it's still missing
        from your build this is a great place to grab it, because you'd be making Chaos Blade regardless.
        You're not spending a meld purely on the ability.</p>
    <p>Everything else on offer:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Shimmering</strong> gives Fire Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Fleeting</strong> gives Reload Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Pulsing</strong> gives Finish Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Soothing</strong> gives Damage Syphon</li>
        <li><strong>Hungry</strong> gives HP Prize Plus</li>
        <li><strong>Abounding</strong> gives EXP Chance</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Level it up before melding it into anything else, as usual.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Chaos Blade as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Chaos Blade, so once it's built and levelled it's yours to keep. No need to sit on
        it waiting for a better use.</p>
</div>

<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>Since this recipe can hand you two different commands, running it through the <a href="/">Command Melding
        Simulator</a> first is worth the ten seconds.</p>

<p>Chaos Blade might be the best value meld Terra has. One recipe, 90% odds, a free run at Once More, and
    nothing else competing for it afterwards. If Sonic Blade is already sitting in your deck, this is your
    next move.</p>
