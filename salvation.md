<h1>How to Get Salvation in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Salvation clears the area around Ventus and heals him while it does it. Getting damage and recovery out
    of a single deck slot is a big deal in a game where you're always short on space. It's
    <strong>Ventus-only</strong>, and thankfully it has one recipe that can't fail, so there's nothing to
    agonise over.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>Single recipe, so the crystal is your only choice. Fortunately it's a good one.</p>
    <p><strong>Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a>.</strong> A recipe that
        can't fail and also hands you Second Chance is hard to argue with. If you're still without it, this
        is one of the cleanest ways for Ventus to fix that.</p>
    <p>The full list:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Shimmering</strong> gives Cure Boost, which is a nice fit since Salvation heals you
            anyway</li>
        <li><strong>Fleeting</strong> gives Reload Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Wellspring</strong> gives Combo Plus</li>
        <li><strong>Soothing</strong> gives Defender</li>
        <li><strong>Hungry</strong> gives Treasure Magnet</li>
        <li><strong>Abounding</strong> gives Lucky Strike</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Get it to max level before melding it away or you'll lose the ability.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Salvation as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Salvation. Once it's made and you've taken your ability off the crystal, it's
        purely a combat command, so there's no reason to be precious about it.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq" id="build-chain">
    <h3>Building the Wind Raid First</h3>
    <p>Wind Raid is the one ingredient you will probably have to make. Both recipes are guaranteed and both
        are Ventus-only, same as Salvation itself.</p>
    <table>
        <thead>
            <tr><th>Meld</th><th>Odds</th><th>Result</th></tr>

            <tr><td data-label="Meld">Treasure Raid + Aeroga</td><td data-label="Odds">100%</td><td data-label="Result">Wind Raid</td></tr>
        </tbody>
    </table>
    <p>Both need an Aeroga, which melds from Aero + Aerora at 90%. If you are also planning on <a
            href="/faith">Faith</a>, make <strong>two Wind Raids</strong>, because that command consumes one
        as well.</p>
</div>
<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>The <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a> will show you which ability each crystal gives before you
    spend your Curaga on it.</p>

<p>Salvation is one of the easier strong commands in Ventus' kit to put together. One guaranteed recipe, a
    free Second Chance on the side, and a command that patches you up while clearing the screen. Worth the
    Curaga.</p>
