<h1>How to Get Triple Burst in Kingdom Hearts Re:coded</h1>
<p><strong>Triple Burst</strong> is a magic command with a memory cost of 16%. The in-game description reads: "Launch a series of swift fireballs toward enemies. The impact will send them reeling." It has 4 recipes. The simplest, <strong>Fira + Fire Raid</strong>, needs nothing more advanced than Fira.</p>

<h2>The Chain Rave Route</h2>
<p>If you have already built Chain Rave for the commands in our <a href="/recoded-best-commands">Best Commands guide</a>, you can use it here. <strong>Chain Rave + Rising Rush</strong> makes Triple Burst. Rising Rush has six recipes, and two of them use Stop (<strong>Stop + Air Spiral</strong> and <strong>Stop + Aero</strong>). It is also an ingredient in <a href="/recoded-eruption">Eruption</a>.</p>
