<h1>How to Get Meteor in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Meteor drops rocks on everything in front of you and it's one of Terra's best magic commands. It's
    <strong>Terra-only</strong>. Three recipes are listed for it, but only one is worth building a plan
    around. The other two are 10% gambles you can safely ignore.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p><strong>Geo Impact + Quake with a Wellspring Crystal gives <a href="/once-more">Once More</a>.</strong>
        Handy that the guaranteed recipe also carries the best ability. A meld that can't fail and hands you
        Once More is one of the better deals in the game.</p>
    <p>The rest of that recipe's set:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Shimmering</strong> gives Fire Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Fleeting</strong> gives Reload Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Pulsing</strong> gives Finish Boost</li>
        <li><strong>Soothing</strong> gives Damage Syphon</li>
        <li><strong>Hungry</strong> gives HP Prize Plus</li>
        <li><strong>Abounding</strong> gives EXP Chance</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Brutal Blast + Zero Graviga happens to share that exact ability set, so even if you do gamble you're
        after the same rewards. Brutal Blast + Magnega is the outlier, giving Combo Plus on Wellspring
        instead of Once More, which makes it the weakest of the three.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Meteor as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Nothing melds from Meteor, so once it's built and levelled you're free to just use it.</p>
    <p>One naming thing that catches people out: <strong>Meteor Crash</strong> and <strong>Meteor
            Shower</strong> are different commands entirely. Meteor Shower is a Shotlock rather than a deck
        command, so it works nothing like this.</p>
</div>

<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>The <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a> will confirm the result and ability before you commit your
    Geo Impact to it.</p>

<p>Meteor is a two-step build with a guaranteed finish and Once More attached to it. Make Geo Impact, level
    it, add Quake. Skip the 10% recipes, because chasing them costs more crystals than just doing it
    properly.</p>
