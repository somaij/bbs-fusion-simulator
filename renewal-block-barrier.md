<h1>How to Get Renewal Block and Renewal Barrier in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>These are the cure-based defensive commands, a Block or Barrier that heals you when you use it. Same idea
    split across characters: <strong>Renewal Block</strong> for Terra and Ventus,
    <strong>Renewal Barrier</strong> for Aqua. All four recipes between them are guaranteed, and two of
    those are one of the friendlier ways to pick up <a href="/exp-walker">EXP Walker</a>.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p><strong>Curaga + Block or Curaga + Barrier with an Abounding Crystal gives <a href="/exp-walker">EXP
                Walker</a>.</strong> This is the headline. EXP Walker levels you up just from walking around,
        and both Curaga recipes hand it over. Since these melds can't fail and the ingredients aren't rare,
        it's one of the least painful ways to get it.</p>
    <p>Those two Curaga recipes have the same ability set as each other across both characters: Dark Screen
        on Shimmering, Magic Haste on Fleeting, Combo F Boost on Pulsing, Air Combo Plus on Wellspring, Item
        Boost on Soothing and Treasure Magnet on Hungry.</p>
    <p>The Esuna recipes are where the characters split:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Esuna + Barrier</strong> for Aqua gives <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a> on
            Pulsing, plus Cure Boost on Shimmering and Defender on Soothing. A strong alternative if EXP
            Walker is already handled.</li>
        <li><strong>Esuna + Block</strong> for Terra and Ventus gives Finish Boost on Pulsing and HP Boost on
            Soothing. Perfectly fine, just less exciting than what Aqua gets.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Level the command before melding it away if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Renewal Block / Barrier as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>Both feed the same two commands when you pair them with another Curaga:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>Slot Edge, from Curaga + Renewal Block or Renewal Barrier, 90%</li>
        <li>Break Time, from Curaga + Renewal Block or Renewal Barrier, 10%</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Careful here, because <strong>the same pair gives both results</strong>. You'll get Slot Edge nine
        times out of ten and Break Time the rest, so chasing Break Time specifically means repeating the
        meld a fair few times. The consolation is that Slot Edge is useful anyway, since it's part of the <a
            href="/blitz">Blitz</a> recipe. A failed Break Time attempt isn't wasted.</p>
</div>

<h3>Want to Test Your Melding Path?</h3>
<p>The <a href="/">Command Melding Simulator</a> will show you which ability you're getting before you spend
    a Curaga on it.</p>

<p>These two are easy to skip past because they're defensive commands rather than something flashy. Don't.
    Four guaranteed recipes, ingredients you'll already have, and a clean route to EXP Walker make them some
    of the more practical melds in the game, whichever character you're playing.</p>
