<h1>How to Get Wind Tracer in Kingdom Hearts Re:coded</h1>
<p><strong>Wind Tracer</strong> is a magic command that takes up 18% memory. The in-game description reads: "Shoot a gust of wind that homes in on enemies and deals continual damage." Most players build it as an ingredient rather than for its own sake, because it feeds five other commands: <strong>Judgment Triad</strong>, <strong>Gravity Drop</strong>, <strong>Cyclone</strong>, <strong>Magnet Grab</strong> and <strong>Tornado Tracer</strong>. There are 4 ways to synthesize it, and Command Matrix results are fixed, so every recipe below always works.</p>

<p><strong>Aeroga</strong> is in three of the four recipes. <strong>Esuna</strong> is in two (paired with Aerora or Aeroga) and has no recipe of its own in the calculator, so it has to be picked up rather than synthesized.</p>

<p>Judgment Triad, Gravity Drop and Cyclone each use up a Wind Tracer, so build as many as the commands you plan to make. The full route to <a href="/recoded-quake">Quake</a> needs three.</p>
<p>One thing to avoid: <strong>Tornado Tracer + Aeroga</strong> makes Wind Tracer, and <strong>Aeroga + Wind Tracer</strong> makes Tornado Tracer. They convert into each other, but each swap spends an Aeroga, so going back and forth only wastes ingredients.</p>
