<h1>How to Get Curaga in Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep</h1>
<p>Curaga is the strongest heal in the game and probably the first thing you should build. It restores most
    or all of your HP in a single use and it stays relevant right through the endgame. Both recipes are
    guaranteed and both work for Terra, Ventus and Aqua, so there is nothing standing in your way except
    finding the Cures.</p>
<p>It is also one of the busiest ingredients in the whole melding system. Eleven separate recipes want a
    Curaga, so plan on making more than one.</p>

<div class="faq">
    <h3>Which Crystal Should You Use?</h3>
    <p>Both recipes are guaranteed, so pick on the ability alone.</p>
    <p><strong>Cura + Cura with a Pulsing Crystal gives <a href="/second-chance">Second Chance</a>.</strong>
        This is the reason to spend the second Cura. Getting Second Chance out of a recipe you were making
        anyway, this early in the game, is one of the best trades available to you.</p>
    <p>Both recipes give <strong>Cure Boost</strong> on a Shimmering Crystal, which makes Curaga itself heal
        for more. That is a tidy little loop if you are building a survival-focused deck.</p>
    <p>The rest of Cura + Cura: Reload Boost on Fleeting, Combo Plus on Wellspring, Defender on Soothing,
        Treasure Magnet on Hungry, Lucky Strike on Abounding. Cure + Cura differs on three of those, giving
        Magic Haste on Fleeting, Combo F Boost on Pulsing and Item Boost on Soothing.</p>
    <p>Level it fully before melding it into anything else if you want to keep the ability.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq">
    <h3>Commands That Use Curaga as an Ingredient</h3>
    <p>This is where Curaga earns its place. Eleven recipes call for one:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><a href="/salvation">Salvation</a>, from Wind Raid + Curaga, Ventus, 100%</li>
        <li><a href="/renewal-block-barrier">Renewal Block</a>, from Curaga + Block, Terra and Ventus, 100%</li>
        <li><a href="/renewal-block-barrier">Renewal Barrier</a>, from Curaga + Barrier, Aqua, 100%</li>
        <li>Slot Edge, at 90%, from Curaga plus Renewal Block, Focus Block, Renewal Barrier or Focus Barrier</li>
        <li>Break Time, at 10%, from those same four pairings</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Note the overlap on those last two. The same pair gives Slot Edge nine times out of ten and Break
        Time the rest, so if you want Break Time specifically you will be repeating that meld a lot. Slot
        Edge is useful anyway since it feeds <a href="/blitz">Blitz</a>.</p>
    <p>Because so much depends on it, make at least two Curagas before you start spending them.</p>
</div>
