<h1>How to Get Eruption in Kingdom Hearts Re:coded</h1>
<p><strong>Eruption</strong> is a physical command with a memory cost of 18%. The in-game description reads: "Drive your Keyblade into the ground and trigger a blast of light that rockets foes upward." It has a single recipe, <strong>Magnet Grab + Rising Rush</strong>. The route below needs none of the big fire, ice or wind chains.</p>
